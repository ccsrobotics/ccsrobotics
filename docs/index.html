Divisions

What We Do

From arena combat to suborbital rocketry, our students design, test, and compete across multiple STEM disciplines.

Flagship Program

BEST Robotics

Boosting Engineering, Science, and Technology. Students design and build remote-controlled robots within strict resource limits while producing formal engineering notebooks, exhibits, and marketing presentations.

Combat Engineering

Battle Bots

Precision engineering meets arena combat. We build, wire, armor, and pilot custom combat bots focused on destructive power and structural survivability.

Aerospace

Rocketry

Hands-on aerospace engineering. Teams fabricate custom model rockets, calculate motor impulse dynamics, and deploy dual-recovery parachute systems.
