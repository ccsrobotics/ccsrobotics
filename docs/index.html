What We Do

BEST Robotics

BEST (Boosting Engineering, Science, and Technology) challenges students to conceptualize, design, and build a remote-controlled robot from scratch to compete in local and regional hubs. We operate like a small business, competing in engineering notebooks, marketing presentations, and exhibit design.

Battle Bots

Precision engineering meets arena combat. We design, wire, and armor lightweight combat robots, focusing on robust structural integrity and offensive capabilities. 2026 marks our inaugural entry into the Battle Bots competition arena.

Rocketry

Reaching for the skies through aerospace engineering. Our team designs, builds, and launches custom model rockets, focusing on aerodynamics, payload capacity, and safe recovery systems.
