What We Do

BEST Robotics

BEST (Boosting Engineering, Science, and Technology) challenges students to conceptualize, design, and build a remote-controlled robot from scratch to compete in local and regional hubs.

Battle Bots

Precision engineering meets arena combat. We design, wire, and armor lightweight combat robots, focusing on robust structural integrity and offensive capabilities.

Rocketry

Reaching for the skies through aerospace engineering. Our team designs, builds, and launches custom model rockets, focusing on aerodynamics, payload capacity, and safe recovery systems.
